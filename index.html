Privacy & Distribution Policy

1. 100% Offline & Private

Ecal is designed with privacy by default. All loan, EMI, interest, and down payment calculations run 100% locally on your device. No financial data, inputs, or personal information are ever collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.

2. Direct Open-Source Distribution

Ecal is distributed directly through its official GitHub repository (Harrykalsi24/Ecal) outside Google Play. Because of direct distribution, Android requires your one-time permission to install updates.

3. Cryptographic Verification & Security

Every update published on GitHub includes SHA-256 checksums and a cryptographically signed manifest. Ecal verifies that downloaded APK packages originate exclusively from the official repository and match the installed application's signing certificate before launching the Android installer.

4. Google Play Protect Respect

Ecal does not attempt to circumvent, disable, or modify Google Play Protect or any Android security settings. Google Play Protect may inspect updates to protect your device.
